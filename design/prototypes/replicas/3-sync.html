<My Story>

From front ends to the cloud underneath them, one layer at a time.

I started in web development in Abu Dhabi, moved into healthcare backends at CyberSapient, and now run AWS infrastructure at Amdrodd Technologies.

I architected a HIPAA-compliant EHR for 1,000+ providers, and I'm now migrating a multi-tenant SaaS from GCP to AWS with Terraform, OIDC-based CI/CD and a hardened landing zone.

I learn by doing, and I write it all down on the blog and in Kube by Kube, a free Kubernetes course.
